SafeSpace SG

A safe first step for employees in Singapore who are unsure whether what happened at work counts as harassment.

How to start

Open the chat window on this page and tell SafeSpace SG what happened, in your own words. You can talk it through, ask whether it might count as harassment, or explore your options at your own pace.

Before you chat

  • SafeSpace SG is an AI assistant, not a counsellor or lawyer.
  • You do not need to share any names.
  • Information comes from official Singapore sources, but please check anything important with the relevant agency.

If you are not safe right now

Call 999 or SMS 70999. For someone to talk to, the Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) 24-hour hotline is 1767.
